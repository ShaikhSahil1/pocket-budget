EVERY TOOL, PLAINLY

What each part is for.

No manual-reading energy required.

01 / OVERVIEW

Your monthly cockpit

Available is what remains. Starting includes new funds plus carry-forward. Expenses is total spending. Awaiting is money still owed to you.

02 / CATEGORIES

Zoom into spending

Use Business Trip, Reimbursable, Personal Buying, or Monthly Travelling to filter the table. Search finds names, details, and categories.

03 / MONTHS

Travel through history

Use the arrows or month picker. Older edits recalculate future carry-forward, so your numbers stay connected.

04 / LEAVES

Time off, remembered

Set casual, medical, and comp-off allowances. Add full or half days. Positive unused leave carries forward; negative leave does not.

05 / MINI TOOLS

Convert and plan

Convert currencies using public daily rates. Build travel plans with notes, packing lists, tasks, and progress—without leaving Monthly Pocket.

06 / PROFILE

Your control room

Open your Sheet, load or save cloud data, download a full JSON backup, restore it, or log out. CSV export lives on the monthly toolbar.

SHEET RECOVERY

Deleted your Google Sheet?

Your paid access can be restored. Your budget records need the Sheet, this browser's local copy, or a downloaded backup.

01

Check Drive Trash first

Restore Pocket Budget — My private data within 30 days. Your cloud budget and licence return together.

Open Google Drive Trash ↗
02

Restore paid access

If the Sheet is gone forever, sign in and choose Already paid? Restore access. Enter your original Razorpay pay_… ID.

We verify the payment, then create a new private Sheet and signed licence.
03

Bring your budget back

On the original browser, use Save to Google. On another device, restore a downloaded JSON backup.

If neither copy exists after permanent deletion, the old records cannot be recovered.
Keep it recoverable.Renaming the Sheet is safe. Do not delete it, and use Download full backup regularly.

SAFE CLEAN-UP

Clearing a month has guardrails.

  1. Choose budget records, leave records, or both.
  2. Download a backup if you may need the data later.
  3. Type the exact YYYY-MM to confirm.

Only the selected month is cleared. Other months and recurring leave allowances stay intact.
