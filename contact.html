1. Email support

Write to minisoftwareshop@gmail.com for help with:

  • Google sign-in or your private Google Sheet

2. What to include

To help us answer quickly, include the Google account email you use with Monthly Pocket, the device and browser, and for payment questions your Razorpay payment ID (starts with pay_).

Please never send passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs, full card numbers, CVV or your budget records.
