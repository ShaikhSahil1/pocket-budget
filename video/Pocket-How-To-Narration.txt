POCKET — PROFESSIONAL HOW-TO VIDEO

01. Meet Pocket
Welcome to Pocket, a private monthly budget workspace designed to make money management feel clear and calm. In this guide, we will walk through every major feature: secure Google sign-in, monthly balances, expenses, reimbursements, leave tracking, mini tools, backups, and data controls.

02. Your account. Your Sheet.
Start by selecting Open Pocket, then sign in with Google. Pocket uses your verified Google account to keep workspaces separate. It creates or finds one private spreadsheet in your Google Drive and writes your workspace to the Pocket Data tab. Pocket does not keep a separate central database of your budget records. A working copy remains in this browser, so use a trusted browser profile on shared devices.

03. Read your month at a glance
The Overview is your monthly command center. Available balance shows what remains after expenses and received reimbursements. Starting balance combines this month's new funds with any positive amount carried forward. Total expenses summarizes the selected month, and Awaiting reimbursement shows money that has not yet returned. The chart explains where your money went, while the daily allowance turns the remaining balance into a practical daily guide.

04. Set the money available this month
Choose Set monthly balance and enter only the new funds received for that month. Do not add last month's remaining money yourself. Pocket automatically carries forward positive unused balances, including across skipped months and year boundaries. Negative balances remain visible in their original month but are not carried into the next one.

05. Add, organize, and find expenses
Select Add expense, then enter a name, amount, date, category, and optional details. Categories include Business Trip, Reimbursable, Personal Buying, and Monthly Travelling. Save the record to update every total immediately. Use the category navigation to focus the table, search by name or details, edit a record when something changes, or delete it when it is no longer needed.

06. Track money that should come back
For a refundable purchase, enable This expense will be reimbursed, or use the Reimbursable category. Pocket counts the cost as an expense and adds it to Awaiting reimbursement. When the money arrives, select Pending refund to mark it Received. Pocket then adds that amount back to the available balance exactly once.

07. Navigate, export, and clear safely
Use the month arrows or month picker to review any period. Export CSV downloads the selected month's expenses together with new funds, carry-forward, and the combined starting balance. Clear month lets you remove the selected month's budget records, leave records, or both. Pocket shows record counts, offers a backup first, and requires the exact year and month before deletion. Other months and recurring leave allowances are preserved.

08. Track time off beside your budget
Open Leaves and set monthly allowances for casual, medical, and comp-off leave. Half days are supported. Casual leave repeats from the month you set it until the next change, while medical and comp-off credits apply only when entered. Add a leave for each date, include details or a reason, and edit or delete it later. Positive unused leave carries forward, while negative balances do not.

09. Control sync and portable backups
Select your profile in the sidebar to manage the Google connection. Open my Google Sheet takes you directly to the private spreadsheet. Load from Google replaces the local workspace with the cloud copy, while Save to Google sends the local copy to the Sheet. Download full backup creates a portable JSON file, and Restore backup replaces the workspace after confirmation. Log out hides the account's data on screen and ends the Google connection.

10. Choose a display currency
Use the currency menu to change how budget amounts are displayed. You can save the choice to your account, use it temporarily until reload, or cancel. Changing the display currency does not convert existing budget amounts. For conversion, open Mini tools and choose Currency converter. Enter an amount, select the two currencies, swap direction when needed, and refresh the public daily rate. Your budget records are not sent to the rate provider.

11. Plan trips without another app
In Mini tools, choose Travel planner. Create a named plan and add free-form notes for booking details or ideas. Packing and Tasks are separate checklists, each with its own progress. You can add, rename, complete, or delete individual items, edit the plan, or remove it after confirmation. Travel plans use the same account-specific browser storage and Google Sheet backup as the rest of Pocket.

12. A little more in control
That is Pocket. Start each month with new funds, record expenses as they happen, close reimbursement loops, and keep an eye on the available balance. Use exports and backups whenever you want a portable copy, and remember that your cloud workspace lives in your own private Google Sheet. Open Pocket and make a little more room for what matters.
